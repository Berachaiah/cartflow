ONE RENDERER. Nothing about a conversation is rendered by Jinja — not a row, not a bubble, not a preview. `cartflow-messages.js` draws all of it from `GET /api/messages` and `GET /api/messages/threads/{username}`, and the reason is that every one of those things also arrives over a socket: a new message moves a row to the top of the list, rewrites its preview, and bumps its unread count. A server-rendered row would still need the JS renderer to mutate it, and then Jinja's copy is pure duplication waiting to disagree. So Jinja owns the furniture — panes, tabs, sheets, empty states — and JS owns anything with a message in it. `
