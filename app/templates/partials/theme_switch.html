Icons, not words: a monitor, a sun and a moon are read faster than three labels at 11px, and the three fit in one row at 320px. Each button keeps an aria-label and an aria-pressed, so the meaning is still there for a screen reader and for a browser that never loads Material Symbols — the icon degrades to the ligature name ("desktop_windows"), which is why the label is spelled out rather than left as a bare glyph.
