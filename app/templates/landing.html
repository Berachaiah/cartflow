Why it works

Built for how campus actually buys and sells

storefront

A shop, not a spreadsheet

Prices, stock and pickup points live on the listing. The same numbers show in the feed, the shop and the product page — so a tag can never quote a price the shop doesn’t have.

auto_awesome

Stories and posts that link to the product

A drop is a photo with the item tagged. Tap the tag and you are on the listing. No DM to ask what it costs, no screenshot hunting in three chats.

payments

Pay, then collect or get it delivered

Checkout is in the app, with pickup at a place the seller set and delivery areas they actually cover.
