# 1. Purpose

This document defines the strategy for launching CartFlow Version 1.

The objective is not simply to release software, but to introduce a stable, trustworthy platform that students and businesses can immediately begin using.

Version 1 focuses on validating the marketplace model, refining the user experience, and establishing operational processes before expanding to additional universities.

---

# 2. Launch Objectives

The primary objectives are:

- Successfully launch within Bells University.
- Validate student adoption.
- Validate business adoption.
- Validate escrow payment workflows.
- Validate event ticketing.
- Collect actionable user feedback.
- Identify operational issues before expansion.

# 25. Recommendation

To maximize the chances of a successful launch, implement a **Campus Ambassador Program**.

Recruit 10–20 influential students before launch to act as early adopters and advocates.

Responsibilities:

- Invite new users.
- Help businesses onboard.
- Report bugs.
- Demonstrate platform features.
- Gather community feedback.

Reward ambassadors with non-monetary benefits such as profile badges, early access to features, exclusive merchandise, or recognition within the community.

This approach accelerates adoption, improves feedback quality, and creates organic word-of-mouth marketing without requiring a large advertising budget.

---

# 26. Summary

CartFlow Version 1 should launch as a polished, reliable, and community-focused platform rather than as an experimental prototype.

By prioritizing stability, meaningful content, active businesses, and structured onboarding, the platform can establish trust, validate its business model, and build the foundation for expansion across universities and, eventually, across Africa.
