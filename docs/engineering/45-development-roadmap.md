> Version: V1.0
>
> Status: Engineering Documentation

> Last Updated: August 2026

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the long-term engineering and product roadmap for CartFlow.

It outlines the planned evolution of the platform beyond Version 1, providing direction for future development while maintaining architectural consistency.

---

# 2. Vision

CartFlow aims to become the leading campus commerce and social networking platform, enabling students, businesses, and institutions to connect through a secure, scalable, and engaging ecosystem.

---

# 3. Roadmap Principles

Development priorities should:

- Deliver value incrementally.
- Preserve backward compatibility where practical.
- Prioritize stability before expansion.
- Maintain engineering quality.
- Build upon existing architecture rather than replacing it.

# 21. Recommendation

The roadmap should remain a strategic planning document rather than a fixed schedule.

Features should move between versions based on user feedback, business priorities, engineering capacity, and operational experience. Before introducing major new capabilities, the team should first ensure that existing functionality is stable, well-documented, and widely adopted.

Major roadmap revisions should be accompanied by updates to the PRD, architecture documentation, and business rules to keep all project documentation aligned.

---

# 22. Summary

The Development Roadmap provides a long-term vision for CartFlow, outlining how the platform is expected to evolve from a campus marketplace into a comprehensive commerce and social ecosystem.

By prioritizing incremental improvements, architectural consistency, and user value, the roadmap serves as a strategic guide for future engineering and product decisions while allowing flexibility to adapt as the platform and its community grow.
