# 1. Purpose

This document outlines the long-term evolution of CartFlow beyond Version 1.

The roadmap provides strategic direction for product development, infrastructure growth, business expansion, monetization, and ecosystem development.

While Version 1 focuses on validating the marketplace within a single university, future versions transform CartFlow into a comprehensive social commerce and campus services platform serving universities across Africa.

---

# 2. Product Vision

CartFlow aims to become the operating system for university commerce.

Students should be able to:

- Buy products
- Sell products
- Discover businesses
- Attend events
- Build communities
- Make secure payments
- Manage businesses
- Promote services
- Connect with fellow students

All from one platform.

---

# 3. Development Philosophy

Future development follows four principles:

Build only after validation.

Prioritize user value over feature quantity.

Maintain a simple user experience.

Scale infrastructure only when necessary.

# 25. Guiding Principles

Every future feature should satisfy at least one of the following:

- Solves a real user problem.
- Improves trust.
- Simplifies workflows.
- Creates measurable business value.
- Supports sustainable growth.

Features that do not meet these principles should be reconsidered.

---

# 26. Recommendation

The roadmap should be reviewed every six months rather than treated as a fixed plan.

After each major release:

- Review product metrics.
- Analyze feature adoption.
- Gather student feedback.
- Gather business feedback.
- Evaluate infrastructure performance.
- Reassess monetization opportunities.

Future priorities should be determined by real user behavior rather than assumptions. This keeps CartFlow responsive to its community while maintaining a clear long-term vision.

---

# 27. Summary

CartFlow's roadmap represents a gradual evolution from a university-focused social commerce platform into a scalable ecosystem supporting commerce, communities, events, developers, and institutions across Africa.

Each version builds on validated success, ensuring that growth remains sustainable, user-centered, and aligned with the platform's mission.
