> Version: V1.0
>
> Status: Business Documentation

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the revenue model for CartFlow.

CartFlow is primarily a social commerce platform that enables students and businesses to buy, sell, discover products, and organize events within university communities.

Revenue is generated through platform services rather than advertisements.

---

# 2. Monetization Philosophy

CartFlow should generate revenue by creating value for businesses rather than interrupting the user experience.

Core principles:

- No intrusive advertisements
- Transparent pricing
- Simple commission model
- Fair pricing for businesses
- Scalable revenue streams

---

# 3. Primary Revenue Stream

Version 1 generates revenue through platform commission.

Commission applies to:

- Product Sales
- Event Ticket Sales

Platform Commission:

**3.5%**

Example:

Product Price

₦10,000

Platform Commission

₦350

Business Receives

₦9,650

---

# 4. Commission Collection

Commission is deducted automatically before payout.

Flow:

Customer Pays

↓

Escrow

↓

Order Completed

↓

Commission Deducted

↓

Business Paid

No manual calculations are required.

---

# 5. Escrow Revenue

Escrow itself is not a paid feature.

The escrow process is included within the 3.5% platform commission.

Customers do not pay additional escrow fees.

---

# 6. Event Revenue

Event organizers pay the same platform commission.

Example:

Ticket Price

₦5,000

Commission

₦175

Organizer Receives

₦4,825

---

# 7. Business Accounts

Creating a Business Account is free.

Businesses can:

- Sell products
- Create events
- Receive orders
- Build followers
- Access Business Hub

No monthly subscription is required in Version 1.

---

# 8. Student Accounts

Student accounts are completely free.

Students can:

- Buy products
- Purchase tickets
- Follow users
- Create posts
- Send messages
- Save products
- Participate in the community

---

# 9. No Listing Fees

Businesses are not charged to:

- Create products
- Upload posts
- Create events
- Build a catalogue

Revenue only occurs after successful transactions.

---

# 10. No Withdrawal Fees

Businesses are not charged by CartFlow when payouts are released.

Any fees charged by payment providers are separate and determined by the provider.

# 21. Revenue Calculations

Platform Revenue =

Sum of all collected commissions

Business Revenue =

Gross Sales - Commission - Refunds

GMV =

Total value of all completed transactions before deductions.

---

# 22. Refund Impact

If an order is refunded:

Commission handling follows platform policy.

Possible approaches:

- Refund full commission
- Retain processing fees if already incurred

Policy should remain transparent to businesses.

---

# 23. Taxes

CartFlow records all transaction values.

Businesses remain responsible for complying with applicable tax regulations unless future legal requirements dictate otherwise.

---

# 26. Monetization Principles

Every monetization feature should:

Provide value first.

Remain transparent.

Avoid degrading user experience.

Maintain trust between students, businesses, and the platform.

---

# 27. Summary

CartFlow Version 1 uses a simple and transparent monetization model centered around a **3.5% commission** on completed product sales and event ticket sales.

This approach minimizes barriers to entry for businesses while providing a sustainable revenue stream for platform growth. Future versions may expand into premium services, developer APIs, sponsored content, enterprise solutions, and university partnerships without compromising the platform's user experience.
